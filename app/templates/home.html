Weekend Itinerary

Tournament Overview

Join us for an exciting weekend of golf, camaraderie, and competition! Get ready to showcase your skills and enjoy a memorable experience.

Dates: Friday, July 18th - Sunday, July 20th

Location: Johnson Family Cabin, 6625 Midtown Road, Siren, WI 54872

Day 1: Friday, July 18th

Welcome Reception

  • All Evening: Hanging out, playing games, swimming, kayaking, paddle boarding, and maybe a bonfire.
  • 7:00 PM: Dinner

Day 2: Saturday, July 19th

Tournament Day

  • 7:30 AM: Round 1 at Fox Run (tee times at 7:40 AM, 7:50 AM, and 8:00 AM)
  • 12:30 PM: Lunch at the Cabin
  • 2:00 PM: Round 2 at Tjader Acres
  • 6:30 PM: Dinner
  • 8:00 PM: Round 3 at Moose Mulligan
  • 9:00 PM: Hang out at the cabin

Day 3: Sunday, July 20th

Recovery Day

  • Morning: Sleep in, clean up, and pack up.
  • 10 AM: Breakfast at Gary's Rude Cafe
  • After breakfast: Either head out directly from Gary's Rude Cafe, or come back to the cabin to hang out a bit longer. Some people will likely have lunch at the cabin or fast food in Siren on the way home. There are also optional stops in Grantsburg and Rush City for additional rounds of golf or disc golf on the way home if you have the energy.
